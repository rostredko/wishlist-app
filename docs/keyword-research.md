## Data Sources & Transparency

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SC` | Real data from your Google Search Console (90 days, Jan–Apr 2026) |
| `EST` | Calibrated estimate based on: competitor traffic (Giftster 4.2M/mo, Moonsift 615K/mo), UA market size (~30M internet users), and SC impression ratios |
| `SERP` | Based on who actually ranks — observed via live search research |

**Volume tiers:** `🔴 100K+/mo` · `🟠 10K–100K` · `🟡 1K–10K` · `🟢 100–1K` · `⚪ <100`

**KD (Keyword Difficulty):** `Low 0–35` · `Medium 36–60` · `High 61–80` · `Very High 81+`

**Opportunity score:** `(Volume × Intent Weight) / KD`
Intent weights: Transactional=3 · Commercial=2 · Informational=1

## Tools to Verify These Numbers

| Tool | Free? | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Search Console** | Yes | Real data for YOUR site — start here |
| **Google Keyword Planner** | Yes (needs Ads account) | Volume ranges, UA + EN |
| **Serpstat** | Limited free | Best UA/CIS market coverage |
| **Google Trends** | Yes | Seasonal patterns — compare "вішліст" vs "список бажань" |
| **Keywords Everywhere** | ~$10/mo | See volumes while browsing Google — cheapest option |
| **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools** | Yes (own site only) | See all your ranking keywords + gaps |

> **Recommended first step:** Open Google Keyword Planner → set location = Ukraine → run all keywords from the Priority Hit List. Takes 15 min, validates or adjusts every EST volume above.
